Artemis Solar Race Car

Composites Lead | Purdue Solar Racing | 2026 Formula Sun Grand Prix

Project Overview

Artemis was built in 8 months and passed mechanical scrutineering at the 2026 Formula Sun Grand Prix in Brainerd, MN. Artemis is fully solar powered, capable of reaching 55 mph, and weighs just 300 lbs. As Composites Lead, I oversaw the design, manufacturing, and testing of all composite structures. Fun fact: Artemis has an effective MPG of 1,500!

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Composite Manufacturing: Led vacuum resin infusion of the carbon fiber chassis and aeroshell, balancing structural stiffness with a 300 lb vehicle weight target.
  • Structural Design & FEA: Designed and optimized composite ribs and internal structures to meet competition safety factors.
  • Testing & Competition: Directed composite operations and mechanical scrutineering at Brainerd International Raceway.
